Facial Reconstruction

Sculpt, annotate and export a three-dimensional facial reconstruction from witness description, reference imagery or direct capture. All case data is written to local disk.

Annotation Skin marks, wrinkle and pigmentation painting, image decals — conformed to the mesh surface
Appearance Hair, facial hair, brows, lashes, eyes, skin tone — with glasses, masks, earrings, piercings and headwear
Analysis AI description-to-face, reference photo overlay, witness-led variant narrowing, age progression
Output Snapshots, stills, turntable clips and model export — retained with the case record
