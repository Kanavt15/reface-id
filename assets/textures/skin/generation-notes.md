# Additional original skin assets — 2026-09-06

Both PNGs were generated with the built-in imagegen tool and ship locally. No image service is used at runtime. The user's reference was a target for surface realism; no identity, mole, scar or other identifying mark was transferred from it.

## microfold-skin-v1.png

Generated as a new macro cheek image. Prompt summary: original seamless 40 mm square human cheek albedo tile, irregular follicular pores, fine intersecting microfolds and short creases, neutral medium tan with subtle pink/golden variation, uniform cross-polarized illumination; no facial features, hair, moles, scars, freckles, acne, shine, grid, leather pattern, text or labels.

The result was too strongly folded to replace the main pore texture. Only its processed surface-normal channels are used as a weaker regional detail layer. Its generated colour does not tint the face.

## face-colour-v1.png

Generated by editing the app's own frontal head render (Age 30, Wrinkle Depth 55, camera at (0, 0.124098, 4.5), vertical field of view 35 degrees, square frame). Full prompt:

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: front-projected human facial SKIN DIFFUSE/ALBEDO texture for this exact 3D head. Edit the supplied image: preserve EXACTLY the head outline, pose, camera framing, positions and contours of ears, nose, lips, eye openings, jaw and neck. No change of geometry or identity. Replace the current flat clay-like surface with photographic, extremely realistic natural adult human skin. The skin should have real irregular follicular pores with different sizes on forehead, nose and cheeks; subtle delicate short surface creases around the eyelids; three or four natural irregular shallow forehead folds; realistic soft lip vermilion with fine vertical lip furrows; natural uneven rose, tan and muted brown pigmentation. This should feel like a high-quality human head scan's raw albedo map, with the richness of an unretouched photographic face, not a procedural CG material. Keep an otherwise clean complexion with no moles, scars, freckles, acne, spots, beard, stubble or hair. Preserve the existing blank eye surfaces and their exact outlines: do NOT add eyeballs, lashes or eyebrows. Make the skin neutral medium-light warm beige with believable slight capillary colour. Lighting must be diffuse, flat, cross-polarized and evenly exposed across the face and ears: remove the directional light, nose shadow, eye-socket shading, neck shadow and specular shine because the result is an ALBEDO layer that will be re-lit in 3D. Skin-only intrinsic colours and very fine pore/crease occlusion can remain. Avoid deep black creases, painted wrinkle lines, leather, cracked clay, orange peel, uniform noise or visible repeated patterns. Keep the charcoal background and exact head silhouette. One square image, as high resolution as possible, no text or labels. The output is a texture asset, not a finished beauty render.
```

The generated image retains some illumination. Runtime processing removes broad luminance and uses restrained relative chromatic variation and fine detail; it is not treated as measured scan albedo. See README.md for projection and fallback behaviour.
